    Health & Audit

    Technical crawls, PPC account health review, tracking setup, and analytics check.

    Deliverable Action Log & Fix Roadmap
  2. Week 2

    Execution Sprint

    SEO on-page optimization, email sequences, ad copy creation, and schema code.

    Deliverable Live On-Page & Ad Updates
  3. Week 3

    Content & Outreach

    Authoring articles, social media scheduling, PR link outreach, and AI entity sync.

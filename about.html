What makes a professional website design?

Brand-appropriate colors, readable content, clear spacing, and easy navigation across devices, especially mobile.

How long does it take to build a website?

Timing depends on scope, page count, and how readily project information is available.

What do SEO and SEM mean?

SEO is search engine optimization, helping search engines read your website. SEM is search engine marketing, promoting your website through search engines.

What is website hosting?

Hosting stores website files and databases on an internet-connected server. Your domain points to that server; hosting carries a monthly fee.

Who should own my domain name?

Register it in your company’s or its owner’s name. Technical contacts can be your provider. Domains need annual renewal.

Can you write the text for my website?

You supply the base information. Vroooom refines wording for clarity and SEO, then seeks your approval before publishing.

What is digital marketing?

Promoting a business through digital communications, including online advertising, email, and social media.

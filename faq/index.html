Design & content

What makes a professional website design?
  • A color scheme that reflects your company.
  • Carefully chosen contrast, sizing and spacing.
  • Readable content that clearly communicates your message.
  • A website that is easy to use across platforms, especially on mobile phones.
Explore website design ↗
What are some qualities of a great website?
  • It reflects your company, colors, products, services and brand.
  • Visitors can navigate it easily.
  • Fresh, quality content is easy to read, with accessible contact and location information.
  • It includes clear calls to action.
  • It works well on mobile, including a click-to-call phone number.
  • It is optimized for web searches.
How long does it take to build a website?

Timing varies with each project. It depends on the scope of the website, the number of pages needed, and how readily information is available to the designer throughout the design process.

Can you write the text for my website?

We typically ask you to provide the base information about your business. We then make wording changes for clearer communication, search engine optimization and other needs. Before the website goes live, you approve the final written content.

Search & marketing

What does SEO and SEM mean?
  • SEO means search engine optimization: work within your website to help search engines read its information.
  • SEM means search engine marketing. The original guidance describes marketing your website and submitting it to search engines.
  • The original guidance recommends working with a company to set up SEO/SEM for visibility in Google, Bing and other searches for your products or services.

Review note: Confirm the search-engine submission advice and the original blanket recommendation that every website needs a company to handle SEO/SEM.

What does organic search mean?

The original answer describes organic results as the area in the middle of a Google results page, below sponsored pay-per-click listings at the top and above paid listings at the bottom.

Review note: This description of Google's page layout may be outdated; confirm it before relying on a fixed placement of organic or paid results.

Does being linked to Google Maps help your business?

If your business has a physical location outside your home address, the original guidance recommends linking both your address and your website.

Review note: Confirm current location eligibility and address-display guidance before applying this advice.

What is digital marketing?
  • Marketing your business to consumers through the internet and digital communications.
  • It includes online advertising, email, social media and other digital advertising channels.
  • It creates, communicates and delivers value by showcasing your product or service online.
  • It can reach a wider audience than traditional methods.
What is the difference between Google PPC and Google Organic Search?

With Google PPC, you pay when someone clicks your link for a keyword or phrase. The original answer describes organic search as using relevant keywords and phrases in website content and images, then submitting the website to search engines.

Review note: Confirm the original submission-based description of organic search before treating it as current SEO guidance.

What if I do not have a physical address to link in Google Maps?

The original answer says we can link your information to “Google My Business”.

Review note: The product name and guidance for businesses without a public physical address need review against current Google requirements.

Domains & hosting

Why is it important to have my domain name set up correctly?
  • Your domain should be registered in your company's name or the owner's name, rather than someone else's.
  • The original guidance warns that changing registered ownership can take substantial time and work, describing it as almost impossible.
  • Your vendor can be listed as the administrative or technical contact, and those contacts can be changed.
  • The original guidance calls for paying to renew the domain each year.

Review note: Confirm the ownership-transfer claim, contact arrangements and renewal schedule with the registrar; these details may be outdated or depend on the domain.

What is hosting for your website?
  • Hosting stores your website's files and database on a secure, internet-connected server. Visitors with an internet connection can reach the site by entering its address in a browser, whether using mobile data, café Wi-Fi or a home connection.
  • Your domain name points to the server or servers containing your website's files and database.
  • The original guidance describes a monthly fee to keep your website hosted and available online.

Review note: Confirm the current hosting plan and billing interval with our team.

Why is it important to backup your website?
  • Digital data needs to be backed up because your data matters.
  • Backups help restore your website quickly and easily.
  • The original guidance also highlights complete site backups for larger websites on dedicated servers.

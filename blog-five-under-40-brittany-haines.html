Five Under 40: Brittany Haines

It is with great honor and excitement that we very proudly announce that our client, Brittany Haines (President and CEO of Growing Room), was selected as one of Columbus, Georgia’s Five under 40! Brittany is sure to be one of the great leaders and forces for good in the community and we are glad that the entire community notices her potential. We can’t wait to see what she does next!
